HELIX QUANTUM CHIP - CONFIGURATION BLUEPRINT

Section 1: Qubit Architecture & Coherence

The Helix-V superconducting quantum processing unit (QPU) features a planar array of 128 physical transmon qubits arranged in an 8x16 heavy-hexagonal lattice. The average qubit relaxation lifetime (T1) is measured at 145 microseconds, while the dephasing lifetime (T2) is calibrated at 210 microseconds under dilution refrigerator operating conditions of 12 Millikelvins.

Section 2: Quantum Gate Fidelity Rates

The Helix processor utilizes microwave pulses for single-qubit rotations and flux-tunable couplers for two-qubit entangling gates. - Single-qubit Clifford gate fidelity: 99.97% (calibrated via randomized benchmarking) - Two-qubit Controlled-Z (CZ) entangling gate fidelity: 99.82% - Average read-out assignment fidelity: 99.45% - Maximum gate execution duration: 40 nanoseconds

Section 3: Quantum Error Correction (QEC) Threshold

To suppress physical noise below the fault-tolerance threshold, Helix-V implements the Surface Code protocol with a distance parameter of d=5. The physical error threshold for logical state storage is bounded at 0.75%. Below this physical error threshold, scaling the distance d exponentially reduces logical error rates.
